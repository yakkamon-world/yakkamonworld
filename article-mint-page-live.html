The Official Mint Page Is Live: One Link for All Five Waves of the Yakkamon Genesis Mint

By YakkamonWorld · Sep 11, 2026

The last link in the chain is live. marketplace.roninchain.com/launchpads/mints/yakkamon is the official mint page on Ronin Market — the page every one of the five free-mint waves will mint from, starting with Wave 1 on Sunday night. The listing carries the drop’s official name, Yakkamon Genesis Mint, the golden egg as its artwork, and a total supply of 10,000.

Until now the free mint had a date, a whitelist and a guide, but the actual place you would click was “the Ronin Launchpad” in the abstract. It isn’t abstract anymore. Save this exact address today — from here, from the official docs, or by typing it yourself — and mint morning becomes one bookmark and one click instead of a search-engine gamble against look-alike pages.

Do this now. Bookmark the official mint page, then check which waves your wallet can mint in at yakkamon.com/whitelist, and put a little RON for gas in that wallet before Sunday. Waves 1, 2 and 5 open at 00:00 UTC on their day; Waves 3 and 4 at 08:00 UTC — the hours in your time zone.

What the listing says

The page is short, and every line on it matters. In plain terms: this is your first chance to mint a Yakkamon on the Ronin chain; every NFT is minted blind; all of them are revealed together on October 14, when 68 Legendaries and 50 Rares emerge from the drop and you find out which monster is yours; and once early access begins, a minted Yakkamon can be deposited straight into the game. Total supply, printed on the listing: 10,000.

One line of Ronin Market fine print is worth decoding: “Prices and purchase limits may vary between wallets.” That is the marketplace’s standard wording for a wallet-gated drop, and it is exactly how this one works — the page reads your connected wallet against the whitelist, so what you see depends on who you are. For a whitelisted wallet the price is free plus RON gas and the limit is one mint per wave; a wallet on no list gets no mint button at all, no matter how much gas it offers.

What it settles, and the one new word

  • The link itself. Every guide on the internet, ours included, has been pointing at the Launchpad’s front page and telling you to find the drop on the day. Now there is one exact address, and it is the same address for all five waves. The site’s clocks, hero buttons and the yellow ribbon at the top of every page now point straight at it.
  • “Genesis,” officially. The listing names the drop the Yakkamon Genesis Mint. We and the docs have been calling the minted monsters Genesis Yakkamon for weeks; the marketplace listing makes the name official. One founding generation, two doors in: mint an egg this week, or earn a rank on the leaderboard for the airdrop.
  • The numbers, in a third official place. 10,000 total with 68 Legendaries and 50 Rares now appears on the docs, on the whitelist page and on the marketplace listing itself, and all three agree. The listing names only the top two bands — the remaining 9,882 are the Uncommons, per the pool the odds are built on.
  • Blind, in writing, where the trading happens. The listing says every NFT is minted blind. That sentence sitting on the marketplace matters more than it does in the docs: the secondary market for hidden eggs opens the moment the first ones mint, and every buyer sees on the listing itself that no seller can know what is inside — what a hidden egg is worth runs the math.
  • Playable, not just collectible. Once early access begins, the listing says, a minted Yakkamon can be deposited straight into the game. That has been the docs’ answer since August; now it is on the sales page, which is the difference between a promise in the manual and a promise at the till.

The safety part, one more time

Popular free mints attract copycat pages the way this one attracts trainers, and the days before a mint are when they do their work. The rules do not change, but now they have an exact address to hang on:

  • The only mint page is marketplace.roninchain.com/launchpads/mints/yakkamon — check the domain character by character. Bookmark it today; on the day, open the bookmark, not a search result and never a link from a DM, a reply or a “support” account.
  • The mint is free. The only thing you pay is RON gas. Anything asking for a mint price, a “verification deposit” or approval over your whole wallet is not Yakkamon.
  • The wallet that mints is the Ronin wallet on the whitelist — the one the checker lists. Keep only the RON you need for gas in it, and read every wallet prompt before approving.
  • Nobody legitimate ever needs your recovery phrase. Not to mint, not to “fix your whitelist,” not ever.

Before Sunday, in four lines

  • Bookmark the mint page.
  • Check your wallet at yakkamon.com/whitelist — every wave it can mint in, 0x or ronin: form.
  • Hold RON for gas in that wallet, bought before the weekend rather than at wave open.
  • Know your hour — 00:00 UTC for Waves 1, 2 and 5, 08:00 UTC for Waves 3 and 4, in your time zone — and come back for every wave you qualify for.
