    NOT CONFIRMED YET

    The gaps worth tracking — and the reason nobody can build a reliable calculator or roster planner for this game yet.

    • Stat systemStats vary per individual and there are hidden initial values on spawn, but the roll range, which stats exist, and how much of it is visible in game are all unknown.
    • Work cycle numbersCycle duration, stamina cost per cycle, yield per cycle, and sleep recovery rate.
    • Tile numbersHow many tiles a Region holds, what each tile past the first costs, and how many parallel jobs a tile actually adds. Only the 10 Wood + 1 Stone starter price is public.
    • The Region unlock condition“Grow your starting Region” is all the post says. Whether that is a tile count, a quest, a level or a resource payment is unknown.
    • The transfer mechanicMoving resources between Regions is called a core strategy, but nothing says what it costs — time, stamina, a building, a fee — or whether monsters move freely.
    • Upkeep numbersThe degradation rate, the fertiliser recipe, real plot-group sizes and how steeply upkeep scales. Only the 1.6× example figure is public.
    • Full type chartOnly Grass→Wood is confirmed. The complete type list and the resource each unlocks is missing — and so is combat type effectiveness.
    • Final skill countAround two per monster plus passives is under consideration, not decided. The full skill list, extraction cost and respec cost are all unpublished.
    • Real-time battle interactionMechanics that let you influence a fight as it plays out are in testing. If they ship, you won’t be able to skip battles outright — only speed them up.
    • Levelling & traitsXP costs, the real level cap, the full trait list, and how much each level actually adds.
    • Rarity distributionTiers are now clear, but not how many species sit in each, nor the drop rates.
    • Egg mechanicsRare egg breakdowns are separated out, but hatch rates and sources aren’t public.
    • Evolution triggersConfirmed to be per-monster and deliberately hidden. The team has said the game will hint rather than explain, so expect this to stay unknown by design.
    • Utility numbersThe Legendary utilities are published as text only. The radius of a small or large AOE, Bloom’s regeneration percentage, and every Rare and Uncommon utility are still unpublished — the team says values are being balanced up to release.
    • The type chart, part twoThirteen types are confirmed, and the count of weaknesses per type (two for dragons, three for everyone else) — but not which thirteen, nor the chart itself.
    • Mechanic swappingPlayers will act inside combat, but what that action is, how often, and what it costs has not been shown.
    • CoinsHow many coins a tradable unit costs, the burn rates that produce them, and whether coins can be bought. Even the name may change.
    • The trainer raffleOne of each Genesis Legendary is reserved for a raffle. No rules, date or entry condition has been published.
    • Endgame featuresInfinite levels and sinks are planned but explicitly not locked down — they arrive after early access.
