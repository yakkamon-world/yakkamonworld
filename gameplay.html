    NOT CONFIRMED YET

    The gaps worth tracking — and the reason nobody can build a reliable calculator or roster planner for this game yet.

    • Stat systemStats vary per individual, but the roll range, which stats exist, and whether they're visible in game are all unknown.
    • Rarity tiersTier names, how many there are, drop rates, and which rares are the stronger ones inside a tier.
    • Egg mechanicsRare egg breakdowns are separated out, but hatch rates and sources aren't public. Breeding itself is confirmed — with line degradation and no hard cap.
    • Work cycle numbersCycle duration, stamina cost per cycle, yield per cycle, and sleep recovery rate.
    • Full type chartOnly Grass→Wood is confirmed. The complete type list and the resource each unlocks is missing.
    • Levelling curveXP costs, the real level cap, and how much efficiency each level actually adds.
    • Trait listWhich special traits exist, what each changes, and how you obtain them.
    • Battle rulesTurn structure, the move system, type effectiveness in combat, and how gyms work.
    • Monster utilitiesWhat each Genesis Legendary actually does. The team has said these land in September.
