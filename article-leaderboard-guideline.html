Early Access Leaderboard Guideline: How to Climb

Aug 3, 2026

Everything that matters in Yakkamon pre-registration comes down to one number: your points rank. It decides your permanent trainer number, whether you get early access at all, which wave you enter, and whether a Monster NFT lands in your wallet at launch.

Sign-up date decides none of that — it sets your Monster Egg tier and nothing else. Here's what the points are actually worth.

Only the top 100,000 trainers get early access. Pre-registering does not qualify you on its own. Below rank 100,000, you wait for full launch.

Daily nurture: how streaks work

Tap your egg 3 times to nurture it, once a day. Every nurture pays a base 5 points plus a streak bonus — the longer the run, the more each day is worth.

StreakPoints per day
Day 1 – 2+6
Day 3 – 4+7
Day 5 – 9+8
Day 10 – 29+9
Day 30++10
  • One nurture per day — the day rolls over at midnight UTC.
  • You always have 36 hours from your last nurture, so a single missed day need not break the run — though that only covers a whole missed day if you nurtured after noon UTC.
  • Leave it longer than that and the next nurture starts again at ×1.
  • A full week at ×30+ is 70 points.
Ninety days of perfect nurture is about 856 points. That's the whole grind from now to early access — worth knowing before you compare it to anything else on this page.

$FLOWER deposits — the biggest lever by far

Deposits opened Aug 9 at 8:00 PM ET (the docs say 10 August — that's the UTC date for the same moment) and are live now. Every $FLOWER earns a point, and your deposited total unlocks bonus rates on top:

The 3× applies only to the opening week. It drops 0.2× every week after until it rests at 1×, so the same deposit made later is worth substantially less. The size bonus is rated per deposit — the full amount has to land in one transfer. The full schedule →

  • Your deposit address is issued on Aug 9 at 8:00 PM ET.
  • Send any amount on Base or Ronin — minimum 5 $FLOWER.
  • Points land in the week the deposit is confirmed. Deposits carry a weekly multiplier that starts at 3× and decays, plus a size bonus per deposit — so depositing early and in one transfer earns more than spreading it out.
  • All $FLOWER can be withdrawn once you gain access to the game.
Be clear-eyed about this: a large $FLOWER deposit is worth tens of thousands of points — roughly 82× everything you'd earn from ninety days of perfect daily nurture. The top of this leaderboard will be decided by capital, not by grinding. That doesn't make the free actions worthless, and it doesn't put the Legendary bands out of reach either — realistically it's the top 100 that's a paid bracket, not the top 1,000. Referrals are uncapped at 30 points each, so around 165 of them match a 5,000 $FLOWER deposit. What capital buys outright is the very top; the rest is contestable. We're not financial advisors and we're not telling you to deposit; we're telling you what the numbers say.

A, B and C are the same power level. Each letter hatches a different species with a different role — some battle, some farm utility — all at the same tier. The difference is scarcity by design: 100 Egg As, 400 Bs, 500 Cs. So rank 1–100 is a rarer prize than 501–1,000, not a stronger one. More →

Two thresholds matter. Top 5,000 is where a Monster NFT starts. Top 100,000 is where early access starts. Everything in between is a question of which week you get in.

What doesn't count — and what can cost you rank

Referral points are provisional until a final review. These are removed:

  • Unverified accounts — sign-ups that never complete verification.
  • Multi-accounting — alt accounts, throwaway emails, shared devices or wallets.
  • Bots and automation — bulk or scripted sign-ups.
  • Inactive sign-ups — accounts made purely to farm a bonus.
Removed referrals are deducted from your total, which can pull your trainer number down. Serious or repeated abuse can disqualify an account entirely. At 30 points each, chasing fake referrals is a poor trade against the risk.

Before October 1st: get verified

The free mint runs on a verified whitelist. Signing up isn't enough — an unverified account can't mint, however fast you are on the day. Complete verification well ahead of time so you're on the list, and you keep the 250 points too.

The short version

  • Nurture daily, ideally after 12:00 UTC so the 36-hour grace period actually covers a missed day.
  • Clear the one-time quests today — 55 points for a few minutes.
  • Mint on October 1st. 250 points, one click, best value on the board.
  • Refer real people only, and help them get started — 30 each. Your first five are instant; after that your friend needs Discord verified, X verified and 100 points. Fakes get deducted.
  • Deposits dominate the top of the board. Decide for yourself whether that's a game you want to play.
  • Top 5,000 for an NFT. Top 100,000 for early access.
