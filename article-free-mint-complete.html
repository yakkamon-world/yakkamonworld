The Free Mint Is Complete: Sold Out in Wave 4, 365 Bad Eggs Flagged, and What Comes Next

By YakkamonWorld · Sep 17, 2026

That’s it — the Yakkamon Genesis free mint is over. All 10,000 hidden eggs have been minted. The supply ran out on September 17, during Wave 4 · Yakkamon Hunters, which by the published rules ended early the moment the collection was fully minted. And it settles the fifth wave’s fate before its clock ever struck: Wave 5 never opened. It was always “whatever is left,” and nothing is left.

The official mint page is now a sold-out listing. Nobody can mint anymore, in any wave — from here on, the only way into the Genesis collection is the secondary market.

The headline numbers. 10,000 of 10,000 minted · 0 left · sold out during Wave 4 on September 17 · roughly 5,500 wallets holding at sellout · every egg sealed until the reveal on October 14.

How the 10,000 filled, day by day

Day one was the quiet one, exactly as designed: Wave 1 was the only guaranteed wave, and about 993 of the top 1,000 claimed theirs — meaning a handful of guaranteed free mints simply went unclaimed. Then the click races started, and each day took a bigger bite than the last day’s leftovers could survive.

Wave 4 opened at 08:00 UTC on September 17 with roughly 2,955 eggs still on the shelf and burned through them in a matter of hours. Its own fine print — “ends early if the 10,000 sell out” — is exactly what happened.

Two weeks ago we ran the numbers on wave sizes against the whitelist brackets and titled the piece “Wave 5 Probably Doesn’t Exist.” It doesn’t.

The Bad Egg hunt: 365 flagged, and counting

Selling out is not the end of the story, because the team is still working through who minted. Accounts that broke the rules — multi-account farming above all — are being identified, and their minted eggs are being labeled on the NFT itself with a “Status: Bad Egg” trait. The label is the mint-week warning being enforced: the team said during the mint that eggs minted by accounts breaching the multi-account rules would reveal nothing, and a Bad Egg is exactly that — a sealed egg that is already known to be empty on October 14.

At the time of writing there are 365 Bad Eggs — up from 53 on September 15, so the review is very much still running, and the number can keep climbing. Anyone can see the flagged eggs: open the official collection page and filter by Status, at marketplace.roninchain.com/collections/yakkamon?Status=Bad Egg.

  • What it means for the collection — of the 10,000 minted eggs, 9,635 still carry a real reveal as of this writing. Every egg the review flags is one less live lottery ticket in the pool.
  • What it means if you’re buying — a Bad Egg trades like any other sealed egg unless you look. Before paying for any blind egg on the secondary market, open its own marketplace page and check the Status trait. Never buy from a screenshot in a DM.
  • What it means if you minted fairly — nothing. One account, real play, your egg is clean and nothing about the review touches you.
The check takes ten seconds. Every listing on the collection page links the egg’s own item page, and the Status trait sits right on it. If it says Bad Egg, you are looking at a sealed egg that reveals nothing — whatever the seller says.

Where are the 250-per-mint points?

On their way, if the team’s own wording holds. The +250 points per mint are credited to the trainer dashboard “once the minting period has ended” — not when a mint confirms — and with the supply gone, that condition is now met. No exact crediting moment was ever published, so a dashboard that hasn’t moved yet is still normal; nothing needs fixing and nothing needs re-minting. Why your total didn’t move on mint day →

What comes next

With the mint finished, the schedule ahead is the official Important Dates page, plus the trading date published on the collection itself. In order:

  • September 21 — Ronin Market opens for trading. Until then OpenSea is the only live venue, and the team advises against trading before Ronin Market opens.
  • Before the reveal — 1,500 extra hidden eggs. Minted after the free mint and handed out at special events, for 11,500 sealed eggs revealed in total. No event dates or eligibility published yet.
  • October 14 — the reveal. Every egg opens at once: 68 Legendaries (3 Storm, 5 Echo, 10 Ghost, 50 Bloom), 50 Rares and 9,882 Uncommons emerge, and every holder finds out what they minted. Bad Eggs come up empty.
  • Ongoing until the lock — the leaderboard. $FLOWER deposit multipliers keep stepping down weekly, and every rank is still climbable — mint points included — until the leaderboard finalizes.
  • One week before early access — the leaderboard locks. The rank you hold at that moment is the rank your rewards are calculated on.
  • Three days after the lock — the Genesis airdrop. The top 5,000 receive their Genesis monsters, the top 2,000 the Legendary versions.
  • November / December — early access launches. Wave 1 enters on launch day, Wave 2 a week later, Wave 3 a week after that; Wave 4 is confirmed closer to launch. Minted Yakkamon can be deposited into the game from early access.
  • One month after early access — Chapter 0. The first official NFT monster hunt, the first batch of Legendaries, auctions, the in-game marketplace and $FLOWER withdrawals.

The full table, with what each date means for your rank, lives on the Early Access page — and our roadmap read covers the gaps between the lines.
