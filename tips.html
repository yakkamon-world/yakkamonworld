1. SPREAD YOUR $FLOWER DEPOSITS WEEKLY

If you plan to deposit $FLOWER, sending it in weekly slices instead of one lump gets you strictly more — same points, more rewards. The reason is that the leaderboard is really two competitions running at once, and a bulk deposit only enters one of them properly.

The two competitions

 Final rankingWeekly ranking
Based onYour total points, all-timePoints you earned that week
ResetsNever — it accumulatesEvery week
Pays outOnce, at early access in Q4 2026Every single week until then
RewardsLegendary / Rare Monster, early-access waveResource box, food box, raffle ticket, loot box

A bulk deposit is one enormous week followed by nothing. Weekly slices enter you into every weekly race between now and Q4.

Why bulk is overkill

The weekly board doesn't reward you for winning by a wide margin. Finishing first with 60,000 points pays exactly what finishing first with 7,500 points pays. Everything above the line you needed is surplus — and a bulk deposit is almost entirely surplus, spent in a single week.

Same $FLOWER, identical total points — eight shots at the weekly prize pool instead of one. ★ marks a week you're competitive in.

Splitting costs you nothing

The obvious worry is that you'd sacrifice bonus points, since bigger deposits earn better rates. You don't. The bonus rate is set by your deposited total, not by the size of each individual deposit. Send 5,000 $FLOWER in one lump or in eight slices and the rate you end up on is the same, because what the system reads is the total you've deposited.

Those rates apply to the total, however you got there. So the choice between bulk and weekly isn't a trade-off at all — it's the same points either way, and one option additionally enters you into every weekly race.

Practical notes

  • Points land in the week the deposit confirms, so send a slice early in each week rather than at the boundary.
  • Minimum deposit is 5 $FLOWER, on Base or Ronin — so even small stacks can be split several ways.
  • Your deposit address is issued when deposits open on 10 August.
  • All $FLOWER can be withdrawn once you gain access to the game.
  • Weekly points come from every source, not just deposits — daily nurture, referrals and X posts all count toward the same weekly total.
