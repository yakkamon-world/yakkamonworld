1. DEPOSIT $FLOWER EARLY, AND IN SINGLE LARGER TRANSFERS

▶ Read this tip as an article →
Updated 7 August 2026 — this tip previously said the opposite. Until the first dev stream, the bonus appeared to follow your cumulative deposited total, which made splitting deposits across weeks free. The team has since described a different structure: a weekly multiplier that decays, and a size bonus applied per deposit. Both reward depositing early and in bigger single transfers. We've rewritten this tip rather than quietly editing it. What changed →

If you plan to deposit $FLOWER, when you send it and how many transfers you split it into both change how many points you get. Two separate bonuses are in play, and they push in the same direction.

Bonus one: the weekly multiplier decays

Every $FLOWER earns points, and the rate depends on which week you deposit in. The opening week pays 3×, and the rate falls by 0.2× every week until it settles at 1× and stops dropping.

On the second dev stream the team acknowledged deposits may close while the multiplier is still around 1.2×, so the 1.0× floor may never actually be reached. What else changed →

Dates are our own calculation from “3× through the opening week from 10 Aug, dropping 0.2× weekly.” The rates are official; the week boundaries are inferred, so check the deposit panel if a date is borderline for you.

The multiplier applies to points, not your balance. You still hold the same $FLOWER and can withdraw or spend it in-game once access opens.

Bonus two: the size bonus is per deposit

On top of the multiplier, larger deposits earn bonus points. The rate doubles at every step, up to +80% at 50,000 $FLOWER — and it's calculated on the size of a single transfer, not on your running total.

The full amount must land in one go. Split deposits each earn their own smaller bonus — so 25 $FLOWER followed by another 25 does not reach the 50 tier, and ten deposits of 500 earn ten 20% bonuses instead of one 40% bonus.

Both mistakes together. Ten deposits of 500 made after the multiplier bottoms out earn 6,000 points — against 17,000 for a single 5,000 transfer in the opening week. That's a 65% loss on identical money.

What that means in practice

  • Deposit in the opening week if you can. 3× is the highest rate there will ever be, and it falls 0.2× every week after.
  • Send it in one transfer rather than several, so the size bonus lands on the full amount.
  • Don't hold back for a big finish. The old instinct — wait, watch the leaderboard, deposit at the end to secure a rank — is exactly what this structure is designed to punish.
  • Minimum deposit is 5 $FLOWER, on Base or Ronin.
  • Points land in the week your deposit confirms, so send early in a week rather than at the boundary.

One thing that hasn't changed

The leaderboard is still two competitions, and that's still worth playing for:

The difference is that deposits are no longer the way to play the weekly board. Nurture, quests, referrals and X posts all feed your weekly total, and they're spread across the whole window by nature. Log in weekly and claim your boxes — just don't hold $FLOWER back to do it.

Check it against your own dashboard. The rates above come from the official deposit panel, and the maths checks out — but the week boundaries are our calculation, and the team is still tuning things. Confirm in your own deposit panel before sending, particularly if you're close to a week edge.

2. NURTURE AFTER 12:00 UTC, EVERY DAY

▶ Read this tip as an article →

Daily nurture is the main points lever for anyone not depositing, and it pays more the longer your run goes. There's also a grace period built into it — but the grace period only actually protects you if you nurture in the second half of the UTC day. That's the whole tip, and the reason is a quirk of how two separate clocks interact.

What a streak is worth

Tap your egg three times to nurture it, once a day. Each nurture pays 5 points plus a streak bonus that climbs with the run:

Streak dayPoints per day
Day 1 – 2+6
Day 3 – 4+7
Day 5 – 9+8
Day 10 – 29+9
Day 30++10

A full week at day 30+ is 70 points. Run it unbroken from early August to the free mint in mid-September — about 42 days — and daily nurture alone is worth about 526 points.

What breaking it costs

Miss the window and your next nurture starts again at day 1. You don't just lose one day — you drop from 10 points a day back to 6, and it takes 29 days to climb back to the top rate. That rebuild is 44 points you'd have banked by staying on the ladder, on top of the day you missed.

Over 57 daysPoints from nurture
Never broken526
Broken once~474
Broken four times~420

The two clocks

Two rules govern the streak, and they don't line up:

  • One nurture per day, and the day rolls over at midnight UTC.
  • You always have 36 hours from your last nurture before the run resets.

36 hours is a day and a half, which is where the “a single missed day need not cost you the streak” promise comes from. But whether it actually covers a missed day depends entirely on when in the UTC day you tapped.

Same 36 hours, different protection. Nurture before noon UTC and the grace period expires before the next day is over; nurture after noon and it carries you past it.

Why noon UTC is the line

Nurture at time T and your deadline is T plus 36 hours. For that deadline to land past the end of the next day, T has to be at least 12:00 UTC. Tap at 08:00 and your window closes at 20:00 the following day — you're still on the hook tomorrow. Tap at 20:00 and it closes at 08:00 the day after — you can miss a whole day and be fine.

The practical version. In the Americas almost any waking-hours nurture already clears noon UTC, so you're fine by default. In Europe, Asia and Australia it's the opposite — a morning nurture is before the cutoff and leaves you no slack at all. If you're east of London, make it an evening habit.

Practical notes

  • Nurturing late one day and early the next is fine — they're different UTC days, so both count and the run continues.
  • The 36-hour grace is a safety net, not a schedule. Using it every other day means you're nurturing half as often and earning half as many points.
  • Nurture points feed your weekly total as well as your all-time one, so a consistent run helps in both competitions at once.
  • Set a repeating alarm for the same time each evening. That single habit is worth more than any other free action on the board.
  • Be realistic about scale: 526 points over 57 days is a fraction of what a large $FLOWER deposit pays. It's the best free lever there is, not a way to out-earn capital.
