1. GETTING STARTED

Not registered yet?

It takes about a minute and needs only an email address. Registering through our link applies access code YAKKA-UDBVPX, which gets you a Gold Box in game.

SIGN UP FREE ↗

Sign up with just an email to claim a Monster Egg — and nothing else. From there, you earn points by staying active before launch — the more points you earn, the better your rewards when the game opens.

Your points decide three things:

  • Your trainer number — your permanent rank among the founding players.
  • Which wave you join — higher-ranked players get in first when early access opens.
  • What you receive at launch — including a shot at a free Genesis monster (see below).

How to earn points:

  • Tap your egg once a day (bonus points for keeping a daily streak going)
  • Link your Discord and Twitter, and post weekly
  • Refer friends — you earn points for every signup
  • Take part in the free mint (see below) — 250 points
  • Deposit $FLOWER early (see below)
Daily actions add up over time. The earlier you start, the harder your rank is for others to catch up to.

3. REWARD TRACK

▶ The reward track explained →

The reward track runs on a weekly cycle, not on fixed points milestones. Every 7 days, a fresh list of rewards appears for you to claim.

  • Claimed items go straight into your inventory and stay there.
  • Once you have early access, you'll be able to claim them inside the game.
  • Each new list replaces the last one — so anything you don't claim is gone.
Check back every 7 days. Because each list replaces the previous one, logging in weekly is the only way to keep collecting.

4. MONSTER EGG TIERS

Sign-up order decides exactly one thing: which Monster Egg you start with. Tiers are handed out in registration order, and once a tier is full, it's gone.

10,001 – 25,000🥇 Gold
25,001 – 50,000🥈 Silver
50,001 – 100,000🥉 Bronze
100,001 – 500,000Basic
Your Monster Egg is not an NFT. It's an in-game item — nothing to mint, nothing to claim, no wallet needed. It simply appears on your farm the first time you log into Yakkamon.
Sign-up egg tiers are mainly cosmetic. The team has confirmed the tiers function as cosmetic and early-collectible differences rather than as power. Every monster works as a worker for you whichever egg it came from — so a Platinum egg is not a head start on the leaderboard and carries no claim on early access.
Signed up late? You can still finish at the top. A Basic egg holder who earns points daily will finish above a Platinum egg holder who signs up and never comes back. Rank is earned, not reserved.

5. REFERRAL RULES

▶ The new referral rules, and what they cost →

Referrals earn points, but only genuine ones count. Referrals are reviewed before rewards are finalized, and points from them stay provisional until that review.

A referral only counts if the friend is a real, separate person with a verified account who actually engages with pre-registration. Each counting referral is worth 75 points, and referrals are uncapped. Your first five are instant — they pay the moment your friend signs up. From the sixth onward, your friend must link a wallet and deposit 100 $FLOWER of their own before the points land. Linking Discord and X no longer verifies a referral: bot accounts were clearing that bar in bulk. These do not count:

Referrals past your fifth are no longer free. The bar is now a 100 $FLOWER deposit made by your friend, with their own money and their own wallet — not something you can complete on their behalf, and not something a genuinely free-to-play friend will clear. What this costs, and who it hits →
  • Unverified accounts — sign-ups that never complete verification.
  • Multi-accounting — referring yourself via alt accounts, throwaway emails, or shared devices and wallets.
  • Bots and automation — bulk or scripted sign-ups.
  • Referrals 6+ where the friend hasn't deposited — from your sixth referral on, the friend must link a wallet and deposit 100 $FLOWER themselves. Their deposit also earns them points, so it isn't purely a cost to them — but it is real money. Why this changed →
  • Inactive sign-ups — accounts created purely to farm a referral bonus, with no real activity.
Removed referrals are deducted from your total, which can lower your trainer number and your tier. Serious or repeated abuse can disqualify an account from pre-registration rewards entirely.

6. GENESIS MONSTERS

▶ The full airdrop write-up →

When early access opens, the 5,000 highest-ranked players each get a free Genesis Monster — a powerful starter creature that will never be available again to anyone, at any price. The top 2,000 get an even rarer Legendary version.

  • Genuinely powerful — not just a collectible. It's a real head start in the game.
  • Starts with XP — it arrives already leveled up, ahead of monsters caught on day one.
  • Fully yours — you can trade it with other players if you want to.
  • Specialized — some are built for battle, some for gathering resources, some for other parts of the game.
This is not first come, first served. Monster NFTs are awarded on points, not on how early you signed up. Signing up does not reserve one — you have to earn your rank and hold it until the leaderboard locks.

There is no sale. No second window. No way to buy in later. The only route is to finish pre-registration inside the top 5,000 on points. Once the leaderboard locks, this airdrop is closed for good.

7. FREE MINT — 14 SEPTEMBER

Updated 2 September from the official free mint page and the free-mint dev stream. Previously mid-September; before that October 1st.

▶ Every decision from the free-mint stream, graded →

On 14 September a free mint of 10,000 hidden Genesis monster NFTs opens on the Ronin Launchpad. It runs in five whitelisted waves over five days, each open to a different slice of the leaderboard, and it costs nothing beyond the network fee (gas, paid in RON). Your leaderboard rank on 10 September decides which waves you are on the list for.

When14 September, then one wave a day to 18 September
WhereRonin Launchpad — on the Ronin blockchain, the network Yakkamon runs on
CostFree — you only pay gas, in RON
Supply10,000 — 68 Legendaries (3 Storm, 5 Echo, 10 Ghost, 50 Bloom), 50 Rares and 9,882 Uncommons
WhoWhitelisted trainers, by leaderboard rank at the 10 September snapshot — see the waves below
How manyOne NFT per wave per account; you can mint in every wave you qualify for
Reveal14 October — all 10,000 revealed at once

The wave supplies add up to 11,000 against a 10,000 collection — later waves only get what earlier waves left. The dates after Wave 1 are the team’s current plan and were described as tentative on the stream, pending feedback on time zones and durations.

  • Minting earns 250 points — more than three referrals for one click, and it lands on your leaderboard score ahead of the final lock.
  • Get to the rank you want by 10 September. Whitelists are finalised from the leaderboard inside yakkamon.com that day. After that, climbing changes nothing for the mint.
  • Deposit at least 5 $FLOWER if you are counting on Wave 2 or Wave 4. It is fully withdrawable — it exists to make multi-accounting expensive, at the Ronin team’s suggestion — and more than 3,000 of the top 10,000 already qualify.
  • Have a Ronin wallet connected and some RON in it for gas. Base deposits count for points, but the mint is Ronin only.
  • Be online when your wave opens. Outside the guaranteed top-1,000 wave, it is fastest-click: “there is usually more people than that supply”, in Adam’s words, and not everyone gets one.
  • Mint in every wave you qualify for. One per wave per account, so a top-1,000 trainer with a 5 $FLOWER deposit can mint in Waves 1, 2, 4 and 5.
You mint blind. All 10,000 stay sealed until 14 October, when every one is revealed at once. That is before the leaderboard finalises, so you’ll know what you’re holding while you can still climb. Hidden NFTs are tradable before the reveal as well as after — and this collection is the ongoing Yakkamon collection, so new monsters released later appear in it too.
What changed from the earlier plan. The mint used to be described as a verified whitelist with one mint per trainer and no race. It is now rank-gated waves with a set supply per wave, one mint per wave per account, and speed matters in every wave except the first. The 5-Legendary figure from the old docs is gone too: 68 Legendaries are hidden in the pool. Our grades for each of those decisions →
After the reveal, you deposit them into the game. A few weeks after 14 October, or whenever you get access, minted monsters can be deposited into Yakkamon and played. The free mint and the Genesis airdrop are separate things: the airdrop is on final rank and lands automatically, the mint is something you do yourself in your wave.

8. FLOWER DEPOSITS

▶ The official deposit guideline, explained →

$FLOWER is the in-game currency already used across the studio's other games — Yakkamon doesn't launch a new one. Depositing early is like pre-loading credit before the store opens.

Every deposit does two things at once:

  • Saves it for you — it's held and ready the moment you get game access.
  • Earns points now — open now (since Aug 9, 8:00 PM ET), minimum 5 $FLOWER, sent on Base or Ronin. Points are boosted two ways: a weekly multiplier of 3× in the opening week, falling 0.2× each week to a floor of 1× (week 3 held at 2.8× after the week-2 pause), and a size bonus rated per deposit doubling from +10% at 50 $FLOWER to +80% at 50,000. Both favor depositing early, in one transfer. The full schedule →

You're not trading one for the other — you keep the $FLOWER and the points.

Where to get it — $FLOWER is the same ERC20 token on Base and Ronin, at one identical contract address, so neither chain needs bridging. You can earn it free by playing Sunflower Land, or buy it — through a swap on Base, or directly inside the Ronin Wallet. The official deposits page now publishes the contract address and the buy links; verify against it before you swap. Where to get $FLOWER →

Once you're in the game, you can spend it on gear and crafting, or withdraw it with no tax, any time.

For security, small percentage-based withdrawal limits may apply during the first few weeks after launch. This only limits how much you can withdraw at once while things stabilise — not whether you can withdraw at all.
