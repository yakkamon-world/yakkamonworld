YOUR DEPOSIT ADDRESS

This is the address Yakkamon gave you to deposit to — the same one on Base and Ronin. It is not your wallet address.

Where do I find it?
  1. Open your account on yakkamon.com and scroll to QUESTS.
  2. On the EARLY-DEPOSIT $FLOWER row, click the DEPOSIT button.
  3. A panel opens showing the point tiers. Keep scrolling — your address sits near the bottom, under YOUR DEPOSIT ADDRESS · BASE + RONIN.

Safe to share: it only ever receives your own deposits, and anyone can read it off the blockchain anyway.

WHAT EACH BAND TAKES

The airdrop pays by band, not by a sliding scale. Everyone who finishes between 51st and 250th takes home the same Echo; 50th takes a Storm; 5,001st takes nothing at all. Points spent inside a band you have already cleared buy you nothing — only crossing into the next one does.

These lines move every time someone deposits, and they only account for deposit points. The leaderboard that decides the airdrop locks one week before early access — holding a band matters as much as reaching it, because there is no partial credit for finishing 5,001st.

HOW THE POINTS ARE WORKED OUT

THE FORMULA

points = amount × (week multiplier + bulk bonus)

The two bonuses add together, they do not multiply. At 2.8×, a 500 $FLOWER deposit earns 1,400 points plus a 100 bonus — the bonus is 20% of the deposit, not 20% of the points.

The bulk bonus is rated on each transfer on its own: +10% at 50, +20% at 500, +40% at 5,000, +80% at 50,000. Splitting one deposit into several can drop each piece into a lower band. The week multiplier starts at 3.0× and falls 0.2× a week to a 1.0× floor, with windows turning at 02:00 UTC.

Deposits made on both chains are added together, because one deposit address covers Base and Ronin alike. Everything here is read from public blockchain data and recalculated regularly.
