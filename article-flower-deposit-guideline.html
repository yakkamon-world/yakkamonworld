The Official $FLOWER Deposit Guideline, Explained

Aug 10, 2026 · Updated Aug 14, 2026

Yakkamon has published a dedicated $FLOWER deposits page in the official docs. It sets out what a deposit actually does, what happens to your money at launch, and one safeguard that hasn’t been widely discussed.

This is the whole thing in one place — the official guideline, plus the live rate schedule from your trainer dashboard, which the docs page doesn’t include.

1. A deposit does two things, not one

This is the part people most often get wrong, and the docs are unambiguous about it. Every deposit credits your in-game balance and earns you leaderboard points. You are not trading one for the other.

The $FLOWER is held for you and waiting the moment you get access. The points are separate, and they push you toward trainer tiers, earlier access waves and the NFT airdrop.

Worth saying plainly: you are not spending your $FLOWER to buy points. You keep both.

2. What you get back, and when

Once early access opens, your deposited $FLOWER is yours to use:

  • Spend it in-game — gear, crafting, hunting supplies, and the rest of the economy.
  • Withdraw it with no withdrawal tax. The docs are explicit: no lock-up, no penalty for taking it back out.

3. The safeguard nobody’s talking about

Buried in the guideline is the detail most worth knowing before you deposit anything.

Percentage-based withdrawal limits may apply for the first few weeks after launch. The team describes it as a standard safeguard while the project stabilises — it caps how much can be withdrawn in a given period, not whether you can withdraw. Limits are lifted as things settle.

That’s a reasonable measure and a common one — it protects the economy from being drained in the first days. But it does mean your deposit isn’t instantly liquid on day one. If you were planning to deposit now and withdraw the moment access opens, plan for that taking longer than a single transaction.

4. The rates: what the docs don’t tell you

The guideline says bigger deposits earn exponentially more, which is accurate but vague. The actual numbers live in the deposit panel in your trainer dashboard, and there are two bonuses stacked on top of each other.

The weekly multiplier — it decays

Every $FLOWER earns points, at a rate set by which week you deposit in. The opening week pays 3×, and the rate falls 0.2× every week until it settles at 1× and stops dropping.

6. Where to get $FLOWER

Worth saying, since a guide about depositing is not much use if you don’t hold any. There are two routes, and one of them is free.

Earn it by playing

$FLOWER is the in-game token of Sunflower Land, the studio’s other game. You earn it by playing — selling crops, completing deliveries, competing in seasonal chapters. It’s free to start, and it means you can build a deposit without spending anything.

Slower than buying, obviously. But if you’re playing the free-to-play route anyway, it’s the only way to get deposit points without opening your wallet.

Buy it

$FLOWER already exists and trades today — Yakkamon didn’t launch a new token, so there’s nothing new to wait for. The official guideline has since been expanded on this point, and it changes the practical advice: $FLOWER is the same ERC20 token on both Base and Ronin, at one identical contract address on both chains. Deposits are accepted on either. Neither chain needs bridging — on Base you swap for it as normal, and on Ronin you can swap for it directly inside the Ronin Wallet.

Which chain you use is therefore down to where you already hold funds, not to which is the shorter route. If your money is on Ronin — where you’ll want a wallet anyway for the October mint — there’s no longer a reason to move it to Base first.

For anything to do with acquiring and depositing it, work from the official source: the Yakkamon $FLOWER deposits guideline ↗, which now publishes the contract address alongside the swap and block-explorer links for each chain, and the deposit panel in your own trainer dashboard, which carries the current rates and your deposit address.

Never take a contract address or a deposit address from us, from a reply, or from a DM — check the official docs page instead, every single time. Fake tokens using the same name and ticker are one of the most common ways people lose money in this space, and a token that looks identical in a swap interface can be worthless. The docs give one address that should match on both Base and Ronin; if what your swap interface shows doesn’t match it exactly, stop.

One more thing worth knowing: $FLOWER is designed as an in-game currency rather than an investment, and Sunflower Land’s own docs say so plainly. It has a fixed supply, the team took no allocation, and its value moves. Buying it to chase leaderboard points is a decision only you can make.

7. The honest part

We’re not financial advisors and we won’t tell you whether to deposit — $FLOWER is real money, the game hasn’t launched, and that call is yours alone. Everything in pre-registration works without depositing anything, and the top 100,000 early-access cutoff is reachable free.

What we can tell you is what the rules are, and that a large deposit made early is worth tens of thousands of points, which no amount of free play matches. That’s why the very top of the leaderboard is effectively a paid bracket.

Your deposit address comes from your own logged-in account on yakkamon.com. It never comes from a post, a reply, a DM or a link somebody sends you. If anyone gives you an address to send $FLOWER to, it’s a scam — every time.
