Tip: Deposit Your $FLOWER Early, In One Transfer

Updated Aug 7, 2026
This tip previously said the opposite. It used to advise splitting deposits across weeks, because the bonus appeared to follow your cumulative deposited total. The first dev stream on 7 August described a different structure, and the advice reverses with it. We've rewritten it rather than quietly editing. What changed →

If you're planning to deposit $FLOWER, two bonuses decide how many points you get — and both reward the same behavior: deposit early, and in single larger transfers.

The weekly multiplier decays

Every week of the deposit window carries a multiplier. The opening week pays 3× — three points per $FLOWER — and the rate then falls by 0.2× every week until it settles at 1×.

Week 1Week 2Week 3Week 6Week 11+
3.0×2.8×2.6×2.0×1.0×

Which means the same 5,000 $FLOWER is worth 17,000 points in the opening week and 7,000 once the multiplier bottoms out — a 10,000-point difference for waiting.

The multiplier applies to points, not to your balance. You keep the same $FLOWER and can still withdraw it once you have game access. What's tripling is what that deposit is worth on the leaderboard.

The team was explicit about why: they want points earned early, rather than a stampede on the last day before launch.

The size bonus is per deposit

Separately, larger deposits earn bonus points — calculated per transfer, not on your running total.

Single depositBonus rateBonus points
50 $FLOWER+10%+5
500 $FLOWER+20%+100
5,000 $FLOWER+40%+2,000
50,000 $FLOWER+80%+40,000

The rate doubles at every step. Ten deposits of 500 earn ten 20% bonuses; one deposit of 5,000 earns a single 40% bonus, which is worth a thousand points more.

Two half deposits are worth less than one whole one. Sending 25 $FLOWER and then another 25 does not reach the 50 tier. It has to be a single transaction.

What to actually do

  • Deposit as early in the window as you're comfortable with. Week one is worth the most, and it only goes down.
  • Send it in one transfer, so the size bonus applies to the full amount.
  • Don't hold back for a strong finish. Waiting to watch the leaderboard and depositing at the end is exactly what this structure penalises — and the leaderboard gets hidden near launch anyway.
  • Minimum is 5 $FLOWER, on Base or Ronin. Points land in the week the deposit confirms.
Check against your own dashboard. These rates come from the official deposit panel. The week boundaries are our own calculation from “0.2× weekly from 10 Aug”, so confirm there if you're close to a week edge.

None of this is advice about whether to deposit at all. $FLOWER is real money and that call is yours. This is only about timing, for people who have already decided.
