We are not the Yakkamon team. This is an unofficial fan portal. We can’t recover accounts, restore streaks, chase missing points, or help with anything inside the game itself — for that you need the official site. What we can do is fix our own pages, answer questions about how the rules work, and pass on things worth knowing.

Prefer email? Write to yakkamonworld@gmail.com directly — the form sends to the same inbox.

WHAT TO EXPECT

  • Corrections get priority. If something on this site is wrong, we’d rather hear it from you than leave it up. We’ve already rewritten a tip and corrected a rank table this month because the facts moved.
  • Tips get credited. Send us a tactic that works and, if it holds up, it goes on the Tips page with your name on it.
  • Scam reports are useful. Fake sites and impersonator accounts spread fast. If you spot one, tell us and we’ll warn the community.
  • We can’t give financial advice, and we won’t tell you whether to deposit. Please don’t ask.
  • We will never ask for your seed phrase, private key, password or wallet access. Nobody legitimate ever will.
