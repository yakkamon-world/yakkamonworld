New: FAQ Page Answers Your Trickiest Pre-Reg Questions

Jul 30, 2026

We just added a dedicated FAQ page, built by going through every page on this site and pulling out the parts that actually confuse people — not generic questions, the real sticking points.

What it covers

Do you need a crypto wallet? What's the difference between your rank, tier, and wave? And the big one: how is the Free Mint different from the Genesis Airdrop, since they both hand out free monsters but work in completely different ways. There's also a plain-language breakdown of "gas" and what Ronin actually is.

Why we built it

A lot of pre-registration info is scattered across dates, tables, and tiers — useful once you understand the system, confusing before you do. The FAQ exists to answer the "wait, what does that actually mean?" moment before it stops you from registering at all.

Check it out on the FAQ page, or jump back to Pre-registration for the full breakdown.
