The Collection Page Is Live: Where the Yakkamon Genesis NFTs Will Trade on Ronin Market

By YakkamonWorld · Sep 12, 2026

The trading side of the drop now has an address. marketplace.roninchain.com/collections/yakkamon is the official Yakkamon collection page on Ronin Market — the home for every Genesis Yakkamon NFT, and the place they will be viewed, listed, bought and sold once they exist.

This is the same “Yakkamon” collection the Genesis Mint listing already sits inside. The mint page opened last week; now the collection it feeds has its own front door, live and public before a single egg has been minted.

Two official pages, two different jobs. You mint on the Launchpad page, marketplace.roninchain.com/launchpads/mints/yakkamon. You view and trade on the collection page, marketplace.roninchain.com/collections/yakkamon. Bookmark both today — and mint only ever from the Launchpad page.

One page to mint, one page to trade

On Ronin Market a launchpad and a collection are two different things, and it is worth keeping them straight before mint night. The launchpad is the drop mechanism: it is where a whitelisted wallet clicks “mint” when its wave opens, one NFT per wave, free plus RON gas. The collection is the marketplace catalog: it is where every Yakkamon that has been minted lives afterwards — where you browse the collection, open any single item, and place a buy or a sell order on the secondary market.

If you are on a whitelist, your job this week is still the Launchpad page. The collection page is what everyone — whitelisted or not — uses to buy an egg they didn’t mint, or to sell one they did.

What the collection page is for

A collection page is the marketplace’s catalog for one project: it gathers every NFT under the “Yakkamon” name in one place, and it is where the numbers a trader watches will appear — how many have been minted, how many wallets hold them, the lowest price anyone is asking (the “floor”), and what has recently sold. It is also where you open an individual Yakkamon to see its own page, and where the two buttons that make a market — list for sale and buy — live.

None of that has any content yet, because nothing has been minted yet: Wave 1 opens on September 14, so today the collection is an empty shelf with the official name on it. That is exactly why it is worth bookmarking now — it is the page you will refresh the moment Wave 1 mints, to watch the first eggs land.

Where the hidden-egg market will actually happen

Every Yakkamon is minted blind and stays blind until the reveal on October 14 — and the drop is tradable in the meantime. That means there is a real market in sealed eggs for roughly a month, and this collection page is the room it happens in. Every listing carries the same fact on its face: no seller can know whether the egg they are selling holds a Legendary or an Uncommon, because nobody will until the 14th.

We ran the math on what a hidden egg is worth when there was no venue to point at — a pre-reveal price is a lottery-ticket price, and a lottery ticket has an expected value measured in the Uncommons that 98.8% of eggs become. Now that math has an address: the floor price on this page, once eggs are minting, is the market’s live answer to “what is a blind egg worth?” The official economy post said it plainly — the free mint and the airdrop launch on the Ronin marketplace first, the in-game market comes after. This is that marketplace.

What’s there today, and what fills it

  • Today (before September 14): the collection exists and can be opened, but there is nothing to buy — no egg has been minted, so there is no floor, no owners and no sales.
  • From Wave 1 onward: as each wave mints, minted eggs appear here. Sellers can list them, buyers can bid, and a floor price forms — all of it in sealed, unknown eggs.
  • After October 14: the reveal turns every egg into its actual monster, and the collection reorganises around what people actually hold — the 68 Legendaries and 50 Rares become visible and priced individually against the 9,882 Uncommons.

The safety rule covers both pages now

A second official address is a second thing look-alike pages will imitate, so the rule from mint week extends cleanly to this one:

  • The only two Yakkamon pages on Ronin Market are marketplace.roninchain.com/launchpads/mints/yakkamon (mint) and marketplace.roninchain.com/collections/yakkamon (trade). Check the domain character by character, and reach them from your own bookmarks — never from a DM, a reply or a “support” account.
  • You never mint from the collection page. Minting only ever happens on the Launchpad page, when your wave is open. If a “collection” page is offering you a mint, it isn’t the real one.
  • Buying on the secondary market means paying a seller’s price for a blind egg — that is a real cost with no guaranteed reward, unlike the free mint. Only spend what you are comfortable losing, and read every wallet prompt before approving.
  • Nobody legitimate ever needs your recovery phrase — not to mint, not to trade, not to “verify” a listing. Not ever.
