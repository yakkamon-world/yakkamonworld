WHAT IS YAKKAMON?

Yakkamon is a creature-collector game with a twist — it's also about building something. You explore the world to hunt and catch wild monsters, then put them to work gathering resources and farming while you're off doing other things.

The world itself is alive: deserts, volcanoes, and oceans that shift between day and night, and change with the seasons. Every three months, brand-new monsters show up in the wild for players to compete to find first.
