We are not the Yakkamon team. This is a fan-run site with no connection to Thought Farm. We can’t see your account, change your points, or fix anything inside the game. For that you need the official site.

WHAT THIS IS

YakkamonWorld exists because the rules of this game are scattered. Some of it is in the official docs, some is said once on a dev stream and never written down, and some of it — the deposit maths, the rank ladder, what a streak is actually worth — only becomes clear when somebody sits down and works it out.

So that is what this site is: the published rules, in plain language, plus the arithmetic nobody else has bothered to do. Guides for the things people keep asking about, news when something genuinely changes, and a deposit leaderboard built from public blockchain data because the numbers were there for anyone to read.

It is free, it needs no account, and there is nothing to sign into.

WHO WRITES IT

Yakkamon players. We pre-registered like everyone else, we deposit like everyone else, and we have exactly the same information you do — no insider line to the studio, no early access, no private briefings.

That is worth stating plainly, because it cuts both ways. It means nothing here is authorised or approved by anyone. It also means nobody is telling us what to write.

Plenty of what ends up on the site starts with a reader. Tips that hold up get published with the sender’s name on them, and a fair few corrections have come in from people who spotted something before we did.

HOW WE WORK

  • Published sources first. The official documentation, the team’s own announcements and dev streams, and the game itself. When something comes from a stream rather than the docs, we say so — spoken answers get revised more often than written ones.
  • Chain data where it exists. Deposits are public transfers on Base and Ronin, so they can be read and added up rather than guessed at. That is where the leaderboard comes from.
  • We test before we publish. The deposit points formula went up only after we rebuilt a real account’s deposits from chain data and the total matched the game exactly, to the point.
  • Our own reasoning is labelled as ours. Where we are working something out rather than reporting it — what a rank is likely to cost, whether waiting is worth it — it is marked as our reading, not the rules.
  • We say when we don’t know. Dates that haven’t been confirmed, numbers nobody has published, mechanics that have only been hinted at. An honest gap is more useful than a confident guess.
We have been critical of the game, and expect to be again. A fan site that only ever cheers is not much use to the people reading it.

WHEN WE GET IT WRONG

We do, and the rules move underneath us as well — a referral change or a schedule shift can make a page wrong overnight without anybody touching it.

When that happens we fix the page and say what changed, rather than quietly editing it and hoping nobody noticed. If you find something wrong, tell us. Corrections go to the front of the queue, and we would much rather hear it from you than leave it up.

We claim no ownership of any of it, and this site is not endorsed by or affiliated with Thought Farm in any way. Where we are quoting or describing something official, the official source is the one to trust — and it is always linked so you can check it yourself.

Our own writing, the calculations and the leaderboard are our work. Quote them, argue with them, use them in your own guides — a link back is appreciated but not demanded. If you are from Thought Farm and want something here changed or taken down, write to us and it will be.

HOW IT PAYS FOR ITSELF

Barely, is the honest answer. Links to yakkamon.com carry our access code, YAKKA-UDBVPX, which gets you a Gold Box in game and earns us leaderboard points. It costs you nothing, you can ignore it entirely, and it is the only thing this site earns.

What we don’t do: take payment for coverage, accept anything in exchange for a favourable write-up, or let any of it change what goes on the page. The privacy page spells out the rest — including that analytics only run if you say yes.

WHAT WE CAN’T DO

  • Anything inside your account. Recover it, restore a streak, chase missing points, change a rank. Only the official team can.
  • Tell you whether to deposit. We will show you the arithmetic. The decision is yours, and we can’t give financial advice.
  • Promise the rules will hold. This game is still in development. Everything here describes the rules as they stand today.
We will never ask for your seed phrase, private key, password or wallet access, and we will never ask you to connect a wallet. Nobody legitimate ever will. Anyone claiming to be us and asking for that is a scammer.
