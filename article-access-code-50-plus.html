Sunflower Land Players (50+ Bumpkin Level): Get Your Access Code Tonight

Aug 1, 2026

If your Bumpkin Level is 50 or higher, your Yakkamon Access Code unlocks tonight at 8:00 PM ET. Level 100+ opened last night and Level 150+ the night before — this is your turn.

Unlocks 8:00 PM ET tonight (Aug 1). That's 5:00 PM PT, 1:00 AM in the UK, 3:00 AM in Turkey and 8:00 AM in the Philippines — the live countdown on the home page shows exactly how long is left in your own timezone.

Step 1: Find Yakkamon in the Plaza

Head to the Plaza in Sunflower Land and look for the "Yakkamon" sign next to Stella. Click on it to open the access code panel.

The Yakkamon booth sits right next to Stella in the Plaza.

From there, click "Get Access Code" to receive your unique code.

Step 2: Sign up with your code

Once you have your code, go to yakkamon.com and enter it on the "Enter Trainer Code" screen, then hit Confirm.

Enter your code exactly as shown, then confirm to complete pre-registration.

What signing up tonight actually gets you

One thing, and it's worth being precise about it: your Monster Egg tier. Eggs are handed out in sign-up order — the first 10,000 trainers get Platinum, then Gold, Silver, Bronze and Basic as the numbers climb.

It does not reserve you a leaderboard place. Signing up early sets your egg tier and nothing else. Your trainer number, your Genesis airdrop and your access wave are all won on points, earned after you register. A Basic egg holder who shows up daily will finish above a Platinum egg holder who never comes back.

Not 50+ yet, or not on Sunflower Land?

You're not shut out. Level 20+ opens tomorrow, August 2nd, at the same time. From August 5th, anyone can register using a referral code from another trainer — no Sunflower Land account needed at all.

Your Bumpkin Level only affects when your code unlocks. It has no bearing on your rank, so arriving in a later tier costs you a few days of point-earning, nothing more.

Full tier breakdown is on the Pre-registration page, the step-by-step lives in the FAQ, and if you get stuck you can ask in our Telegram chat — send the bot your Bumpkin Level and it'll tell you your unlock time.
