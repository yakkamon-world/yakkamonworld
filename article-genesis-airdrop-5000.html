The Genesis Airdrop: 5,000 Monsters, One Chance

Jul 27, 2026

When early access opens, 5,000 Genesis Monster NFTs will be airdropped to the players who registered earliest and ranked highest. There's no sale and no second window — once the leaderboard locks, the Genesis line closes for good.

Who gets what

Ranks 1–1,000 receive a Legendary Genesis Monster, the rarest tier that will ever be minted. Ranks 1,001–5,000 receive a standard Genesis Monster. Every Genesis creature arrives already carrying in-game XP, and some are built for battle while others excel at gathering.

The reveal

Nobody finds out what they got until a live Genesis Reveal Event at launch, where every Genesis monster is unwrapped at once.
