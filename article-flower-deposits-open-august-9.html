$FLOWER Deposits Open Sunday, August 9 at 8:00 PM ET

Aug 5, 2026 · Updated Aug 10, 2026

$FLOWER deposits open on Sunday, August 9 at 8:00 PM ET. That's when your deposit address is issued, and when deposited $FLOWER starts converting into leaderboard points.

What you can do from that moment

Send any amount of $FLOWER to your deposit address on Base or Ronin, minimum 5 $FLOWER. Every $FLOWER earns a point, and your deposited total unlocks bonus rates on top:

BonusHow it works
Weekly multiplier3× through the opening week, then falling 0.2× every week until it rests at 1×
Size bonusRated per deposit, doubling at every step: +10% at 50 $FLOWER, +20% at 500, +40% at 5,000, +80% at 50,000

Points land in the week the deposit confirms. It's the single biggest points lever in pre-registration — a large deposit is worth tens of thousands of points, which no amount of daily nurturing will match. We're not telling you to deposit; we're telling you what the numbers say.

Why the docs say “10 August”

Because they're quoting the UTC date. 8:00 PM ET on Sunday Aug 9 is midnight UTC, which is already Monday Aug 10 in London and everywhere east of it. Same instant, two calendar dates — if you're in the Americas, the evening you want is Sunday the 9th.

Deposit early, and in one transfer

The instinct is to wait — watch the leaderboard, see where you stand, and deposit near the end to secure a rank. The deposit system is built specifically to punish that.

Two bonuses stack, and both reward moving early. The weekly multiplier pays 3× through the opening week and falls 0.2× each week after. The size bonus is rated per deposit rather than on your running total, so 25 $FLOWER followed by another 25 does not reach the 50 tier — the full amount has to land in one go.

Concretely: 5,000 $FLOWER sent as one transfer in the opening week earns 17,000 points. The same 5,000 sent as ten deposits of 500, after the multiplier bottoms out, earns 6,000. The full week-by-week schedule →
