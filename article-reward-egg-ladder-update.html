The Reward Egg Ladder Just Changed

Aug 12, 2026

Yakkamon has rewritten the reward egg ladder. The bands are tighter, the top 10 now receive two eggs, and there’s a new Rare Egg B tier. Here’s the new table, what it means for where you are, and what the team says about it.

The new ladder

Points rankReward egg
1 – 3🥇 Legendary Egg A + 🥈 Legendary Egg B
4 – 10🥇 Legendary Egg A + 🥉 Legendary Egg C
11 – 50🥇 Legendary Egg A
51 – 250🥈 Legendary Egg B
251 – 500🥉 Legendary Egg C
501 – 2,000Rare Egg A
2,001 – 5,000Rare Egg B
5,001+❌ No reward egg

Only the top 10 receive two eggs. Ranks 11 to 5,000 receive one, decided on points alone.

What the team says

“Nothing has been nerfed.” The team’s stated position is that this update does not nerf an existing Yakkamon for a leaderboard ranking. Lower-tier Yakkamon utility is being lifted to match previously expected traits, and more monsters are being released — so every rank still gets the kind of utility it was already in line for, and far more players end up rewarded with powerful monsters. Monster utilities are due to be published in September.

What changed, in plain terms

Both things are true at once, so here they are side by side.

If you finish at…You used to getYou now get
Rank 2Legendary Egg AEgg A + Egg B
Rank 8Legendary Egg AEgg A + Egg C
Rank 40Legendary Egg ALegendary Egg A
Rank 100Legendary Egg ALegendary Egg B
Rank 300Legendary Egg BLegendary Egg C
Rank 600Legendary Egg CRare Egg A
Rank 3,000Rare Egg ARare Egg B

The bands tightened, particularly in the middle. Total Legendary supply fell from around 1,000 to roughly 510 — about 50 Egg As, 200 Egg Bs and 260 Egg Cs, counting the double awards at the top.

Against that: the top 10 now get two eggs instead of one, Rare Egg B extends rewards deeper than before, and the team says the utility attached to each egg is rising rather than falling.

Our read. Calling it “not a nerf” is fair on utility and questionable on scarcity — if you were sitting at rank 400 expecting a Legendary Egg B, you are now looking at Egg C, and at rank 600 you have dropped out of Legendary entirely. Whether the lifted utility makes up for that is genuinely unknowable until September, because the utilities haven’t been published. We’d rather say that than pick a side on your behalf.

What to do about it

  • If you were targeting a Legendary, the line moved from rank 1,000 to rank 500. That is a materially harder target and worth re-planning around.
  • If you were targeting top 5,000, nothing got worse — you now get Rare Egg B where the old ladder gave Rare Egg A, and both are reward eggs.
  • If you are outside 5,000, the position is unchanged: no reward egg, but early access still runs to rank 100,000.
  • Nobody should act on utility assumptions yet. The one number that decides whether this is good or bad for you lands in September.

The full rank ladder and the pre-registration guide are both updated, and the early-access wave bands are unchanged — this update covered reward eggs only.
