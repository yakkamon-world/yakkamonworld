## Official mint page on Ronin Market — "Yakkamon Genesis Mint" (live since September 11, 2026)

Source: https://marketplace.roninchain.com/launchpads/mints/yakkamon

Key facts in plain terms, from the official mint listing on Ronin Market (the page itself is the source of record; it is a JavaScript app, so this summary is what it shows): the official mint page for the Yakkamon free mint is live at https://marketplace.roninchain.com/launchpads/mints/yakkamon. The listing is titled "Yakkamon Genesis Mint," under the Yakkamon collection, with the golden egg as its artwork. This is the one and only official mint page — every one of the five free-mint waves (September 14–18, 2026) mints from this same page. Anyone asking where to mint should be given this exact address and told to bookmark it and open it from the bookmark on mint day, never from a link in a DM or a reply, because look-alike pages appear around popular mints.

What the listing states: total supply is 10,000. It is the first chance to mint a Yakkamon on the Ronin chain. Every NFT is minted blind, and all of them are revealed on October 14, 2026, when 68 Legendaries and 50 Rares emerge from the drop and each minter finds out which monster they got. Once early access begins, a minted Yakkamon can be deposited straight into the game — so minted monsters are playable, not just collectible. The listing also carries Ronin Market's standard fine print that prices and purchase limits may vary between wallets: this is a wallet-gated drop, so what a visitor sees depends on the connected wallet — a whitelisted wallet mints free plus RON gas, one mint per wave, while a wallet on no whitelist cannot mint at all. Which waves a given wallet can mint in is shown by the official checker at yakkamon.com/whitelist, not by the mint page. The wave-by-wave table on the listing renders live in the app; wave names, supplies, eligibility and opening times are as published on the whitelist page (00:00 UTC for Waves 1, 2 and 5; 08:00 UTC for Waves 3 and 4).

## Free mint whitelist page (yakkamon.com/whitelist, official, live since September 10, 2026)

Source: https://yakkamon.com/whitelist

Key facts in plain terms, from the official whitelist page (the page itself is the source of record; it is a JavaScript app, so this summary is what it shows): the free mint whitelists are published and final. The page has a checker: paste a Ronin wallet address in either the 0x… form or the ronin:… form and press Search, and it lists every free mint wave that wallet is whitelisted for. The snapshot behind the trainer waves (Waves 1, 2, 4 and 5) was taken on September 10, 2026 at 01:00 UTC; rank changes or deposits after that time do not change the lists. Wave 3 comes from the 90-day Ronin on-chain activity snapshot that ended September 8.

Every wave now has an official name and an official opening time: Wave 1 "Top Trainers" opens September 14, 2026 at 00:00 UTC, 1,000 supply, for ranks 1–1,000 at the snapshot, guaranteed — the wave's supply matches its whitelist exactly. Wave 2 "OG Trainers" opens September 15 at 00:00 UTC, 3,000 supply, for ranks 1–10,000 with at least 5 $FLOWER deposited, first come first served. Wave 3 "The Ronin Wave" opens September 16 at 08:00 UTC, 2,000 supply, for active Ronin gamers from the 90-day snapshot across six games — Axie Infinity, Pixels, Craft World, Ronkeverse, Moku and Sunflower Land — first come first served. Wave 4 "Yakkamon Hunters" opens September 17 at 08:00 UTC, 5,000 supply, for ranks 1–20,000 with at least 5 $FLOWER deposited, first come first served; it ends early if the 10,000 supply is fully minted. Wave 5 "Public Trainers" opens September 18 at 00:00 UTC for ranks 1–50,000 with no deposit needed; it gets only what the earlier waves left and may never open if the supply is gone. In local terms, 00:00 UTC is 8:00 PM the evening before in New York and 5:00 PM in Los Angeles; 08:00 UTC is 4:00 AM in New York, 9:00 AM in London and 4:00 PM in Manila.

One mint per eligible trainer per wave, and being in an earlier wave does not use up your place in a later one, so a wallet whitelisted for several waves can mint once in each. The wallet that mints must be a Ronin wallet — the one the checker lists; a Base address cannot mint. The page does not show how many mints remain in a wave, does not show your leaderboard rank, and does not give a closing time for a wave; the reveal is October 14, 2026 with no hour published. Whether the checker already applies the 5 $FLOWER condition for Waves 2 and 4, or only the rank, is not stated on the page. The page supersedes the candidate CSV lists that the Ronin Wave docs page linked to: the checker is the way to see whether a wallet made the Ronin Wave.

## Wave 3 — The Ronin Wave (official page on docs.yakkamon.com, published September 8, 2026)

Source: https://docs.yakkamon.com/pre-registration/free-mint/ronin-wave

Key facts in plain terms, from the official page (the page itself is the source of record): Wave 3 of the free mint opens on September 16, 2026 (at 08:00 UTC, per the whitelist page) with 2,000 spots reserved for active Ronin gamers. No Yakkamon rank, leaderboard position or $FLOWER deposit is needed for this wave. The team took a 90-day snapshot of on-chain activity ending September 8 across five Ronin games: Axie Infinity (Atia's Blessing activations, holding Mystic Axies, Axie marketplace buys and sells, any Axie NFT transfer) — 20,040 wallets; Craft World (DynoCoin/COIN activity on Katana) — 14,422; Pixels ($PIXEL staking and claiming in Pixels and Pixel Dungeons, holding or trading Farm Land, holding or trading Pets) — 4,247; Ronkeverse (holding or trading Ronkeverse NFTs, $RONKE token activity) — 3,658; Moku (holding or trading Moki Genesis NFTs) — 2,354. Deduplicated across the five games: 42,730 unique wallets. The published lists are candidates, not the final whitelist; the final cut of 2,000 is made from these lists plus a whitelist supplied by the Ronin team; being on a list is a strong position, not a guarantee. Lists were finalized on September 10 — since then the checker at yakkamon.com/whitelist is the final word for any wallet, and Sunflower Land is listed there as the sixth game. Sunflower Land players get their own spot in this wave; the team said its list would count active farming on a Ronin farm over the same 90-day window, $FLOWER activity on Ronin, holding Sunflower Land collectibles, Bumpkins and Farm NFTs, and marketplace trades.

How to check (superseded on September 10 by the checker at yakkamon.com/whitelist, which accepts 0x or ronin: form): open a list (the mega list master_deduped.csv covers every wallet and all games; there are ranked per-game lists for Axie Infinity, Craft World, Pixels, Ronkeverse and Moku, hosted in the sunflower-land/yakkamon-docs GitHub repository under ronin_waves/) and search for your Ronin wallet address in lowercase 0x… format, not the ronin: format. Columns: rank (position in that game's list, lower is better), score (activity, higher is better), active_days_90d (days on-chain in the window), and in the mega list games and games_count (which games you appeared in and how many); later columns are the raw signal counts.

How the score works: active days across the 90-day window are the biggest input; signals are weighted and capped per day, so repeating an action many times in one day does not beat playing across many days; cross-game players sort to the top of the mega list; bot-like wallets (automated flipping, spam) are heavily discounted and rank last but stay in the file, flagged; custodial, guild and escrow wallets are discounted; contracts (marketplaces, DEXes, staking contracts) and a batch of sybil wallets are excluded entirely.

Caveats from the page: Pixels VIP is bought from an in-game custodial balance and is invisible on-chain, so it is not counted; holding NFTs without transacting for 90 days shows as 0 active days and a low score; Craft World creates an embedded smart wallet that may not be the address you would mint from, so Craft World players should check their Ronin Wallet address; Moku Grand Arena entries are not counted yet; only on-chain activity counts.

What happens next: September 10 whitelists finalized (snapshot taken 01:00 UTC); September 14 the free mint goes live on the Ronin Launchpad (marketplace.roninchain.com/launchpads); September 16 at 08:00 UTC Wave 3 opens with 2,000 supply; connect the same Ronin wallet the checker lists, keep a little RON for gas, and mint; October 14 every Genesis NFT is revealed together. One mint per wallet per wave — trainers who also qualify through the leaderboard can mint in those waves too. The snapshot is only for the Ronin free mint and does not affect the trainer airdrop leaderboard, which keeps running and finalizes separately. Not on the list? The 5 $FLOWER route into Waves 2 and 4 closed with the September 10 snapshot; what remains is the marketplace from September 14, the 1,500-mint ecosystem airdrop and the top-5,000 pre-registration airdrop.

## Yakkamon — Hunting (official post by the Yakkamon team on X, early September 2026)

G'day trainers. Back with another guide — today we're heading into the hunting grounds.

Hunting in Yakkamon isn't about walking into tall grass and hoping. Every wild Yakkamon has a place, a time and a temperament — and catching them all means studying the grounds, learning the clock, and figuring out what each monster actually wants from you.

1. Hunting grounds are scattered across your Regions, and each one reveals different Yakkamon at different times of day. What roams a ground at dawn won't be what you find there at midnight — learn the clock and plan your hunts around it.

2. You don't hunt alone. Send your Yakkamon out to passively roam a hunting ground, and they'll trigger encounters with the wild monsters living there while you get on with running your farm.

3. Not every encounter goes the same way. Some wild Yakkamon are affectionate — they'll only come home with you if the Yakkamon you sent wins them over.

4. Some are greedy. They want resources, and they won't budge until you've paid up — so bring the right goods if you're chasing a particular monster.

5. And some are just angry. Cross their path and it's a battle — send a Yakkamon that can't hold its own and you'll come back empty-handed.

6. Legendaries and Rares will sporadically appear in the hunting grounds. There's no schedule to memorise here — you'll want a strong, well-supplied team ready to go the moment one shows up.

7. Hunting grounds aren't infinite. The more you hunt from one, the less populated it becomes — so spreading your hunts across grounds and Regions is part of the strategy.

8. And it'll keep changing. Future Seasons will affect which Yakkamon are available and where, so the hunting map you master today won't be the one you're working next Chapter.

Key facts in plain terms: hunting grounds sit inside Regions; each ground shows different Yakkamon at different times of day (dawn vs midnight); you send your own Yakkamon to roam a ground passively and they trigger encounters while your farm keeps running; wild Yakkamon have three temperaments — affectionate (won over by the Yakkamon you sent), greedy (want resources paid before they join) and angry (a battle — a weak Yakkamon comes back empty-handed); Legendaries and Rares appear sporadically with no schedule; grounds deplete the more you hunt them, so spread hunts across grounds and Regions; future Seasons change which Yakkamon appear where.
