PRIVACY

The short version: we count visits if you let us, the contact form emails us what you type, and that’s the lot. Nothing is sold, and there are no ads.

WHAT WE COLLECT

Two things, and only two:

  • Anonymous visit statistics, through Google Analytics — but only if you agree when asked. If you decline, nothing is loaded and nothing is sent.
  • Whatever you type into the contact form, which is emailed to us so we can reply.

We don’t have accounts, logins or profiles. There is nothing here to sign into, so there is no database of you to keep.

ANALYTICS

We use Google Analytics 4 to see which pages people actually read. That genuinely shapes what gets written — the deposit guides exist because the deposit pages were the most-read on the site.

It records things like which page you landed on, roughly where in the world you are, and whether you came from search, X or Telegram. Your IP address is anonymized. We can’t see who you are, and we never try to.

Nothing loads until you say yes. Google Analytics sets cookies, so we ask first rather than after. Decline and the script is never loaded on any page — not a blocked request, not a silent one. None.

Google’s own handling of that data is covered by their privacy policy.

THE CONTACT FORM

The contact form passes your message through Web3Forms, which emails it to our inbox at yakkamonworld@gmail.com. That means your name, email address and message pass through their servers on the way to us.

We keep those emails so we can follow up, and we don’t add you to any list. If you’d rather skip the form entirely, email us directly — same inbox, one fewer party involved.

WHAT WE DON’T DO

  • No ads, and no advertising trackers of any kind.
  • No selling or sharing of anything, to anyone, ever.
  • No cross-site tracking, no advertising pixels, no fingerprinting.
  • No embeds. YouTube videos are linked, never embedded — so YouTube doesn’t see you unless you click through.
  • No wallet connection. We will never ask you to connect a wallet, sign a transaction, or enter a seed phrase. Anyone claiming to be us and asking for that is a scammer.

We’re an unofficial fan project. We have no access to your Yakkamon account, your points or your deposits, and we can’t change anything in the game.

YOUR CHOICES

You can change your mind about analytics at any time:

Checking your current setting…

Turning it off removes the script from every page on this site immediately. Your browser’s own “do not track” and ad-blocker settings work here too — we don’t attempt to defeat either.

Want your contact-form message deleted from our inbox? Ask us and we’ll delete it.

One thing worth repeating. Your Yakkamon deposit address comes only from your own logged-in account on yakkamon.com, and nobody legitimate will ever ask for your seed phrase. Not us, not the team, not support. More on staying safe →
