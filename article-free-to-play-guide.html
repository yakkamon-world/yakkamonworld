The Free-to-Play Guide to Early Access

Aug 5, 2026

You can reach early access without spending anything. You cannot reach the top of the leaderboard without spending — a 50,000 $FLOWER deposit is worth 70,000 points, which is more than a hundred days of perfect free play. This guide is about playing the game that's actually winnable: hitting the thresholds that matter, on a routine that costs about two minutes a day.

The two thresholds

Everything between them is a question of which wave you get in, not whether you get in at all.

RankWhat you get
1 – 10Two eggs — Legendary A plus B or C. A paid bracket, realistically
11 – 50Legendary Egg A — the scarcest, roughly 50 in existence
51 – 250Legendary Egg B
251 – 500Legendary Egg C — the last Legendary band, and the stretch target for a free player with real reach
501 – 2,000Rare Egg A — a serious but achievable free target
2,001 – 5,000Rare Egg B — comfortable on the routine below
5,001 – 100,000Early access, waves 3 – 4. No reward egg
100,001+No early access

Set your target by how much reach you have. Top 5,000 is where a reward egg starts and is comfortably achievable on the routine below. Top 2,000 upgrades that to Rare Egg A. A Legendary now means top 500 — a much tighter band than before the 12 August update, but still not paid-only, since referrals are uncapped.

Do today — about an hour

These are worth 55 points and they never come round again. More importantly, one of them gates the single best reward on the board.

  • Get verified. Not optional and not the same as signing up. The October 1st free mint runs on a verified whitelist — an unverified account can't mint no matter how fast you click. Do this first, today, so there's time to fix problems.
  • Link a wallet — 10 points. You'll need a Ronin wallet for the mint anyway, so this kills two birds.
  • Connect X — 10 points. Also unlocks the weekly X post quest.
  • Join the Discord — 10 points.
  • Post in Discord five times — 5 points each, capped at 25. Do the five, then stop; further posts earn nothing.
  • Start your nurture streak today. Every day you delay is a day the streak isn't climbing.

Every day — two minutes

Tap your egg three times. That's the whole routine, and it's the backbone of a free run: about 526 points between now and the free mint.

  • Do it after 12:00 UTC. The 36-hour grace period only covers a whole missed day if you nurtured in the second half of the UTC day — the reasoning is here.
  • Streaks pay 6 points a day at the start and 10 a day from day 30. Breaking one drops you back to 6 and costs about 44 points climbing back.
  • Set a repeating alarm. This single habit outperforms every other free action except referrals and the mint.

Every week — five minutes

  • Post on X — 20 points the first time, rising with your posting streak. Over eight weeks that's 160–220 points for about forty minutes of total effort.
  • Check the weekly board. Weekly rank resets every week and pays a resource box, food box, raffle ticket and loot box. A free player earning 70–100 points in a week is competing against everyone else's weekly total, not their all-time one — which makes weeklies far more winnable than the final ranking.

The one date that matters: October 1st

250 points for one click. The free mint is the best value on the entire board — the same as eight referrals, or half of everything else a free player earns in two months. You pay only the Ronin network fee, a few cents. If you do exactly one thing from this guide, do this. You must be verified beforehand.

Referrals are the whole free game

This is the part most free players underrate. Every other free action is capped by the calendar — you can't nurture twice a day or post twice a week. Referrals aren't capped at all. At 30 points each they scale as far as your reach does, and they are the only reason a Legendary band is open to a free player: about 165 valid referrals is worth the same as a 5,000 $FLOWER deposit.

Your first five referrals are instant. From the sixth onward your friend must verify Discord, verify Twitter/X and reach 100 points before the points land — a sign-up alone pays nothing. That puts the work in onboarding rather than broadcasting: send them the one-time quest list and tell them to nurture daily and they'll clear 100 in about a week. The rule change →

Here's what a realistic run to October 1st actually looks like, with 50 referrals:

Referrals are 60% of a realistic free total. Everything else combined is the other 40%.

Real people only, and they have to play. After your first five, a referral only counts once that friend has verified Discord, verified Twitter/X and reached 100 points. Referral points stay provisional until a final review. Unverified sign-ups, alt accounts, shared wallets, bots and dormant accounts all get stripped out — and removed referrals are deducted from your total, which can pull your rank down. Serious abuse disqualifies the account. At 30 points each, faking them is a bad trade.

What to ignore

  • Discord posting past the 25-point cap. It pays nothing after five posts.
  • Chasing the top 100. That band is decided by 50,000-scale deposits — 70,000 points, or roughly 2,300 referrals. The bands below it are genuinely contestable; this one isn't.
  • Points beyond a band edge. Rank 4,999 and rank 1,500 get identical rewards. Once you're comfortably inside a band, extra points do nothing until the next one.
  • Raffle tickets as a plan. Nice when they land, but never something to organize your week around.

The whole guide in six lines

  • Get verified today — the free mint depends on it.
  • Clear the one-time quests today: 55 points, ten minutes.
  • Nurture daily, after 12:00 UTC, and never break the streak.
  • Post on X once a week.
  • Mint on October 1st. Best value on the board.
  • Refer real people. Uncapped, 60% of your final total, and the only route to a Legendary band without depositing.
